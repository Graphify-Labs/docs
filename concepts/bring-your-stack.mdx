Graphify indexes the GitHub repositories and GitLab.com projects you connect. Hosted Graphify lists 63 grammars. It also reads docs, PDFs and images alongside your code, so your coding agent can use them.

See [Supported inputs](/open-source/features/languages) for the languages and formats Graphify can map, and the [Quickstart](/platform/quickstart) to connect a repository.
