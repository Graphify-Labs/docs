Invite people from **Members**. Choose **Invite**, enter their email, and pick a role. On hosted Graphify they get an email or a one-time accept link. Invites expire after 48 hours. **Resend** issues a new link. The full roster, including who can approve an access request, is on [Workspaces and members](/platform/workspaces-and-members).

A private deployment does not send that email. The person joins at their next Okta sign-in, or you copy the invite link and send it yourself.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full control, including invites, access requests, seats, and the billing portal. One Owner per workspace. On hosted Graphify, ownership cannot be transferred by changing a role. |
| **Admin** | Invite people, manage members, and view pending access requests. Can see what the workspace is charged. Cannot approve or deny requests, open the billing portal, or see invoices. |
| **Member** | Use repositories, graphs, reviews, Ask, and agents. Cannot invite or manage members. |
| **Billing** | Open the billing portal, manage payment, buy seats, and change how often you pay. Cannot manage members. |

Owners, admins, and billing members can read the audit log. Members cannot. See [Usage and audit](/enterprise/usage).

Owners and admins mint and revoke API tokens. When a member leaves, Graphify revokes the workspace API tokens that member created.

Hosted Teams and Enterprise bill a seat when an invitation is accepted. Seat prices are on [Plans and pricing](/platform/support/pricing). A private deployment has no seat limit.

## Repository teams

Repository teams exist on a private deployment. A team is a group of people with leads. You assign repositories and cross-repo groups to a team. A member sees the repositories, graphs, wikis, insights, and memory of their teams, and not the rest.

Leads add and remove members. An invitation or an API token can be limited to a team.

Postgres row-level security enforces this. A request that has no team scope sees nothing.

Hosted Graphify does not use repository teams. On hosted Graphify, members of the workspace can open the repositories in that workspace.
