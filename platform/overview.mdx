Graphify is the hosted product at app.graphify.com. We run it for you, so there's nothing to deploy. You use it through the console, the web UI at [app.graphify.com](https://app.graphify.com). Your work lives in a workspace: a personal one for you, or a team workspace you share. Graphify brings repository graphs, wiki, pull request reviews, formal verification, Learning, and agent memory into that workspace for your team and coding assistants.

To keep everything on your machine instead, use Graphify Local, the open-source `graphifyy` package. Start with the [local quickstart](/open-source/quickstart).

## Start with your goal

For workspaces in the personalized-discovery rollout, workspace creation includes one optional question: **What would you like to do first?** You can choose agent-assisted development, understanding code, reviewing changes, sharing knowledge, product integration, or exploring Graphify. Leaving it blank keeps a balanced introduction.

**Overview → Explore Graphify** explains available capabilities and opens their destinations. Your goal changes recommendation emphasis; it keeps the sidebar and feature access stable. **Settings → Your experience** lets you change your goal, optionally add a work role, or choose **Only when I ask** to suppress unsolicited suggestions and automatic tours. Preferences are personal to you in each workspace.

Structure and pattern discovery are rolling out separately and require a compatible index. Missing coverage is shown explicitly. Graphify Cloud, Graphify Local, and self-hosted installations can expose different capabilities; follow the availability shown by the installation you are using.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step titleSize="h2" title="Open your workspace">
    Go to [app.graphify.com](https://app.graphify.com). Sign in with Google, GitHub, GitLab, or email. If you are new to Graphify, select **Create an account** from the login page. On the login page, **Forgot your password?** starts a reset. That link is on login, not on signup.
  </Step>
  <Step titleSize="h2" title="Connect a repository">
    Follow the repository setup in your workspace. Graphify builds a graph of the repositories you connect. Use **Overview** to see your graphs, indexing status, and recent builds.
  </Step>
  <Step titleSize="h2" title="Explore the relationships">
    Open a graph to inspect its nodes and connections. Use the cross-repository view when you need context across services and shared code. The [console tour](/platform/console) walks through the product screens.
  </Step>
  <Step titleSize="h2" title="Connect your coding agent">
    Use **Connect agent** in the console, or follow the [client-specific MCP instructions](https://graphify.com/mcp). The hosted connection uses an OAuth sign-in so your assistant can access your Graphify workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Graphify and Graphify Local

Graphify manages the hosted workspace for your connected repositories. The [open-source quickstart](/open-source/quickstart) runs the graph engine on your machine and writes the output into your project. Its CLI commands and local MCP setup are documented separately in these docs. To compare the two, see [Graphify vs Local](/platform/graphify-vs-local). To move a repository, follow [Move from Graphify Local](/platform/support/move-from-local).

Formal verification is part of Graphify. Available features, permissions, and usage limits depend on your workspace and plan; see [plans and pricing](/platform/support/pricing) for hosted allowances.

For self-hosting, SSO/SAML, GitHub Enterprise, and procurement requirements, see [Enterprise](/platform/support/pricing#enterprise).
