Free, Pro, and Teams are plans for Graphify, the hosted product at app.graphify.com. We run it for you, so there's nothing to deploy. Enterprise is a separately scoped offering for organizations that need their own deployment and commercial terms.

<Note>
  Prices and hosted allowances below match the current [pricing plans](https://graphify.com/pricing?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=link&utm_campaign=platform-support-pricing), updated November 13, 2026. Check your workspace's **Billing** page and checkout for the plan and offer available to you. Enterprise pricing and capacity are agreed with your account team.
</Note>

## Choose a plan

| Plan | Best fit | Where it runs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | One developer getting started, with no card required. Up to 3 indexed repositories. | Graphify |
| **Pro** | One developer who needs uncapped graphs and higher build, review, and verification limits. | Graphify |
| **Teams** | A team sharing repository graphs, central billing, and a dedicated Slack support channel. Minimum 2 seats. | Graphify |
| **Enterprise** | An organization requiring self-hosting, enterprise identity, GitHub Enterprise, and negotiated terms. | Your infrastructure; deployment scoped with Graphify |

[Open-source Graphify](/open-source/quickstart), the `graphifyy` package on your machine, is a separate, free Apache 2.0 tool. It runs locally without a Graphify account. A hosted subscription is not required to use it.

## Subscription prices

All prices are in USD. You can pay monthly, quarterly or yearly. Quarterly saves about 15% over monthly, and yearly saves 30%. Yearly prices are shown per month, but you're charged once a year.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Quarterly billing | Annual billing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| **Pro** | \$25/month | \$64/quarter | \$17.50/month equivalent (\$210/year) |
| **Teams** | \$40/seat/month | \$102/seat/quarter | \$28/seat/month equivalent (\$336/seat/year) |
| **Enterprise** | Custom quote | Custom quote | Custom quote |

Teams needs at least **2 seats**, so the smallest yearly Teams plan is \$672 a year.

If you subscribed before these prices, you keep the price you have now.

### Free trial

Pro and Teams include a **14-day free trial** the first time your workspace subscribes. Each workspace gets one trial. Start it at [signup](https://app.graphify.com/signup?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=link&utm_campaign=platform-support-pricing) or from **Billing** (a card is required for the trial). When the trial ends, the plan renews as a paid subscription unless you cancel before then; a cancelled trial returns the workspace to Free. Upgrading an existing Pro subscription to Teams doesn't start a new trial.

The Free plan stays free with no card, within the allowances below.

## Hosted plan allowances

Usage is counted at the scope shown in each row. Workspace allowances are shared across the workspace; the pricing page does not describe them as a separate allowance for every purchased seat.

| Allowance | Free | Pro | Teams |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Indexed repositories | 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited shared |
| Graph nodes per repository | 25,000 | Uncapped | Uncapped |
| Push-triggered graph builds per repository | 10/day | 100/day | 100/day |
| PR reviews per workspace | 15/month | 400/day | 700/day |
| Concurrent PR reviews | 2 | 10 | 20 |
| Formal verification runs per workspace | 1/day | 400/day | 800/day |
| Concurrent formal verification runs | 1 | 2 | 2 |

**Uncapped** applies to graph size on Pro and Teams. Build, review, verification, and operational limits still apply.

On Free, if a repository's newest build goes over the graph-node allowance, Graphify keeps serving the last graph that fit and shows **Showing an older graph** on that repository's graph page until you upgrade. Agents connected over MCP, including Claude, read from that older graph, so their answers can miss your latest changes. To upgrade, open **Billing** in your Graphify workspace and choose Pro.

Pro also reads your documents in depth and gets early access to the Jira, Confluence, Sentry AI SRE, Google Drive and Vercel connectors. Sentry AI SRE drafts fixes from Sentry issues. Each connector opens to paid workspaces as it becomes ready, and some aren't ready yet, so check [Connectors](/platform/connectors) for what you can use today. Teams has everything in Pro, plus shared graphs, one bill for the team and a **dedicated Slack support channel**. See the [Graphify overview](/platform/overview) for graphs, Wiki, reviews, formal verification, Learning, and agent memory.

### Inactive Free workspaces

A Free workspace that is more than 7 days old pauses after 7 days with no use. While it's paused, Graphify stops indexing, linked-graph builds, and PR reviews for that workspace.

<Info icon="circle-pause">
Pausing doesn't delete anything. Your existing graphs stay readable, and you can resume at any time.
</Info>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What counts as use" icon="activity">
    Any of these keeps a workspace active: a member signing in to the app, an agent calling the hosted MCP server, or someone asking a question in Ask.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How to resume" icon="play">
    Open **Repositories** and click **Resume** on the **Workspace paused** banner. Owners, admins, and members can resume; billing-only members can't. A paused workspace doesn't resume by itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens after you resume" icon="refresh-cw">
    Graphify queues a catch-up build for each repository that changed while the workspace was paused, so your graphs come back up to date.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Pro and Teams workspaces don't pause for inactivity.

### Build concurrency

All hosted plans allow **1 index build at a time per repository** and **10 across a workspace**. PR reviews and formal verification runs have the plan-specific concurrency limits shown above.

### Rate and memory limits

Ask, query, and MCP requests have per-minute limits, and agent memory has daily and per-repository limits. They are the same on every hosted plan. See [Rate limits and plan limits](/platform/support/rate-limits) for the numbers, what a `429` looks like, and how to retry.

## Enterprise

Enterprise runs Graphify on your own infrastructure, with enterprise identity and negotiated terms. It is in **early access**. Deployment, capacity, and price are scoped with your account team, not bought at checkout.

| Area | What Enterprise includes |
| --- | --- |
| Deployment | Self-hosted on your infrastructure. Bring BYOC, VPC, on-premises, or air-gapped requirements to the scoping call. |
| Repositories | Unlimited. Capacity is sized for your deployment; the hosted allowances above are not Enterprise guarantees. |
| Identity and code host | SSO/SAML and GitHub Enterprise support. |
| Workflows | Repository graphs, graph-aware PR review, and formal verification. |
| Support | A dedicated Slack support channel. Response times, coverage hours, and any SLA are agreed in the contract. |
| Commercial | Per developer seat, custom quote. Custom invoicing, payment terms, DPA, and terms of service. |

### Data and compliance

In a self-hosted deployment, ingested code and graph data stay in your infrastructure, and retention follows your own policy. Plan the whole data path: code hosts, identity provider, model endpoints, connected assistants, and CI. An external coding assistant still follows its own provider's terms.

Graphify does not train models on your code. Graphify's SOC 2 Type II engagement has started, and the audit is in progress. The [Trust Center](https://trust.graphify.com) lists the engagement letter, the October 2026 penetration test report, controls and subprocessors. See the [Security page](https://graphify.com/security?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=link&utm_campaign=platform-support-pricing) and [Security and data handling](/platform/support/security) for the hosted service.

### Prepare for the scoping call

| Bring | Why it helps |
| --- | --- |
| Developer count and expected growth | Scopes seats and rollout phases. |
| Code hosts, repository count, languages, and sizes | Sets integration and indexing requirements. |
| PR volume and CI or verification workflows | Sizes workload and concurrency. |
| Cloud or on-premises environment, regions, network limits | Defines deployment and data residency. |
| Identity provider and access rules | Scopes SSO, administration, and offboarding. |
| Security questionnaire, audit needs, retention policy, DPA | Gives your reviewers a concrete list of controls to check. |

A typical evaluation runs in four steps: scope the deployment, agree success measures on representative repositories, pilot with one team, then complete security review and procurement before you add more teams.

## Billing and subscription management

Open **Billing** in your [Graphify workspace](https://app.graphify.com?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=link&utm_campaign=platform-support-pricing). It has four tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Your current plan, what you pay, and **What Graphify did** in this workspace. |
| **Usage** | Your limits, how much of each you've used, and when each one resets. |
| **Plans** | Free, Pro, Teams, and Enterprise side by side, with a monthly or annual toggle. |
| **Invoices & payment** | Your card and billing details, and past invoices. |

A dot on **Usage** means a limit is near or reached.

### Overview

The current plan shows what you pay. For owners, admins, and billing members on a paid plan, this is the amount Stripe will charge, including any discount. A discount appears on its own **Discount** line with its name and terms, and the price before the discount is struck through. If Stripe can't be reached, the page shows the list price and says so, with **Retry amount**.

The plan also shows a short renewal line when it applies:

- **Trial ends:** and the date during a trial.
- **Next charge** with the amount and date, or **Ends at the end of this period** if you've cancelled. Owners, admins, and billing members see this on a paid plan.
- **Next renewal:** and the date, for other members on a paid plan that's still set to renew.
- **Canceled. Does not renew** after the subscription has ended.

A Free plan with no subscription omits this line.

On Free, Overview lists your allowances with what you've used, such as **2 of 3** repositories, and marks any that are **Near limit** or **At limit**. It suggests Teams when more than one person is using the workspace. **Compare plans** opens **Plans**.

### What Graphify did

**What Graphify did** counts this workspace's activity over the last 7, 14, or 30 days. Pick the range in **Time range**.

| Tile | Counts |
| --- | --- |
| **PR reviews** | Reviews Graphify completed in this workspace. |
| **Agent queries** | Agent calls and graph queries from everyone in this workspace. |
| **Repos kept current** | Repositories with a fresh index in this period. |
| **Memories saved** | Shared memories saved for the team, and how often agents recalled them. |

Each tile opens the page behind it. A new workspace sees three first steps instead: **Index a repository**, **Connect your agent**, and **Get your first PR review**.

Below the tiles are two cards about your own agents:

- **Your agents called Graphify.** How many times your agents called Graphify in the last 7 days, across all your workspaces. With no calls yet, it links to **Connect your agent**.
- **Token savings.** How many tokens your agents' Graphify answers used in this workspace, compared with reading the same source files directly. It is not your model bill. It shows **Not measured yet** until your agents have queried Graphify here.

### Usage

**Usage** shows PR reviews, verification runs, index builds, and group builds, each with what's left and when it resets. It also shows your graph size against your plan's cap. Limits reset on their own. At a limit, new work waits for the reset. Reaching a limit never adds a charge.

On Teams, **Team** shows **Developers** (seats used out of seats bought), **Using agents** (teammates who have made at least one agent call), and **Active** (teammates who used Graphify in the last 30 days).

### Who can do what

| Action | Owner | Billing | Admin | Member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See the plan, usage, and activity | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| See the amount Stripe will charge | Yes | Yes | Yes | No, sees the list price |
| Open **Manage billing** (the Stripe billing portal) | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| See invoices | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| **Switch to annual** | Yes | No | Yes | No |
| Upgrade, buy seats, or **Manage teams** | Yes | Yes | No | No |

If you don't have access, the button stays visible but greyed out, and hovering it shows **Only the owner or a billing member can change billing.** Invoices show **Only the owner or a billing member can see invoices.** When every Teams seat is in use, only the Owner can add a seat while inviting someone or approving an access request.

Use the billing portal for payment details, invoices, and cancellation.

### Failed payment

If a paid payment fails, the workspace stays open during a grace period. A banner explains what to do and, when the backend has a deadline, when to update the card. Owners and billing members can update payment; everyone else is asked to check with an owner.

### Cancel a paid plan

Paid subscriptions renew until cancelled. Under the standard [Terms of Service](https://graphify.com/terms?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=link&utm_campaign=platform-support-pricing), cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and paid fees are non-refundable except where required by law. After that, the workspace stays usable on the Free plan (within Free allowances). If a Teams subscription has ended, **Billing** shows **Resubscribe to Teams**. The page says your members and shared graphs are still here. Choose how many developers to restart it with. Owners and billing members can resubscribe. Everyone else sees **Ask the workspace owner to resubscribe to Teams.** Review the confirmation shown when changing a plan or seat count. Enterprise invoicing and changes follow the agreement with your account team.

## Common questions

### Do I need Enterprise for formal verification?

No. Formal verification is also part of Graphify, with the hosted allowances listed above. Enterprise adds the deployment, identity, support, and commercial arrangements described in [Enterprise](#enterprise).

### Should we choose Teams or Enterprise?

Choose Teams for a hosted workspace with shared graphs, central billing, and a dedicated Slack support channel. Discuss Enterprise if you need your own infrastructure, SSO/SAML, GitHub Enterprise support, or negotiated procurement terms.

### Does buying more seats increase every usage limit?

The listed allowances have repository, workspace, and request scopes. Do not multiply workspace limits by seat count. Confirm any additional capacity with Graphify before planning a larger rollout.

### Where do we start?

Start on Free, or start a 14-day Pro or Teams trial, at [signup](https://app.graphify.com/signup?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=link&utm_campaign=platform-support-pricing). Free needs no card. **Billing** is where you review a plan you already have. For self-hosting and enterprise requirements, [contact the enterprise team](https://graphify.com/enterprise?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=link&utm_campaign=platform-support-pricing).
