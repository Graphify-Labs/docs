**One workspace you own. Others you join.** Your account always has a workspace you own. Teammates invite you into theirs, or you request access when they already connected your GitHub organization.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full control, including inviting people, approving or denying access requests, buying seats, and opening the billing portal. Each workspace has exactly one Owner, linked to the account that owns it. Ownership can't be transferred by changing roles or approving a request, and the Owner can't leave or be demoted. |
| **Admin** | Invite people, manage members, and view pending access requests. Can see what the workspace is charged. Cannot approve or deny requests, open the billing portal, or see invoices. When every seat is in use, Graphify asks you to have the Owner add one. |
| **Member** | Use the workspace: repositories, graphs, reviews, Ask, and agents. Cannot invite or manage members. |
| **Billing** | Open the billing portal, manage payment, and buy seats. Cannot resume a paused Free workspace or manage members. |

Owners and admins manage the member roster. Only owners and billing members can open the Stripe billing portal and see invoices on **Billing**. See [who can do what on Billing](/platform/support/pricing#who-can-do-what).

## Invite teammates

Member management is available on **Teams** workspaces. Free and Pro personal workspaces stay single-player until you move to Teams.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    In your [Graphify](https://app.graphify.com?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=link&utm_campaign=platform-workspaces-and-members) workspace, open **Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an invite">
    Click **Invite**, enter their email, and choose **Admin** or **Member**. Each person uses one seat. When every seat is in use, only the Owner can add one by sending the invite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They accept">
    They get an email (or a one-time accept link you can copy). After they join, you can change their role to Admin, Member, or Billing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Teams invitations expire after **48 hours**. **Resend** issues a new accept link and starts a fresh 48-hour window. If the invite adds a paid seat when it is accepted, only an Owner can resend it. An expired invite returns an error on accept; send a new invite or resend from **Members**.
</Note>

A pending invite that will add a paid seat is marked **Adds a paid seat on accept**. You're only charged if they accept. If a seat frees up first, they take that seat and nothing is charged. A declined or expired invite also costs nothing.

## Request access

If a teammate already installed Graphify on your GitHub organization, connecting that org can offer **request access** instead of creating a second workspace. Only the Owner can approve or deny pending requests, on **Members** and in **Settings**. Admins can see them.

## Switch workspaces

When you belong to more than one workspace, the sidebar workspace switcher lists every workspace you can open. Pick one to move there. Agents keep their own workspace selection over MCP with `list_workspaces` and `set_workspace`. See [Graphify MCP](/platform/graphify-mcp).

## Last active on Members

The **Members** roster shows **Last active** for each person. It combines console use and agent calls into one line. Hover for the split between Graphify, agent, and pull request activity.
