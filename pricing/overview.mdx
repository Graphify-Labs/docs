Graphify offers hosted plans for individual developers and teams, and a separately scoped Enterprise offering for organizations that need their own deployment and commercial terms.

<Note>
  Prices and hosted allowances below match the current [pricing plans](https://graphify.com/pricing), updated October 8, 2026. Check your workspace's **Billing** page and checkout for the plan and offer available to you. Enterprise pricing and capacity are agreed with your account team.
</Note>

## Choose a plan

| Plan | Best fit | Where it runs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | One developer getting started, with no card required. | Graphify Cloud |
| **Pro** | One developer who needs uncapped graphs and higher build, review, and verification limits. | Graphify Cloud |
| **Teams** | A team sharing repository graphs, central billing, and a dedicated Slack support channel. Minimum 2 seats. | Graphify Cloud |
| **Enterprise** | An organization requiring self-hosting, enterprise identity, GitHub Enterprise, and negotiated terms. | Your infrastructure; deployment scoped with Graphify |

The [open-source CLI](/quickstart) is a separate, free Apache 2.0 tool. It runs locally without a Graphify account. A hosted subscription is not required to use it.

## Subscription prices

All prices below are in USD. Annual prices are monthly equivalents; the annual amount is charged once per year.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | $0 |
| **Pro** | \$15/month | \$10/month equivalent (\$120/year) |
| **Teams (first 100 teams offer)** | \$29/seat/month | \$20/seat/month equivalent (\$240/seat/year) |
| **Teams (standard price)** | \$40/seat/month | \$28/seat/month equivalent (\$336/seat/year) |
| **Enterprise** | Custom quote | Custom quote |

Teams requires at least **2 seats**. At the introductory annual rate, that is \$480/year for two seats; at the standard annual rate, it is \$672/year. Availability of the first-100-teams offer is confirmed at checkout.

Pro and Teams include a **two-week trial**, with a card required to start. Free has no card requirement.

## Hosted plan allowances

Usage is counted at the scope shown in each row. Workspace allowances are shared across the workspace; the pricing page does not describe them as a separate allowance for every purchased seat.

| Allowance | Free | Pro | Teams |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Repositories | 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited shared |
| Graph nodes per repository | 25,000 | Uncapped | Uncapped |
| Push-triggered graph builds per repository | 10/day | 100/day | 100/day |
| PR reviews per workspace | 15/month | 400/day | 700/day |
| Concurrent PR reviews | 2 | 10 | 20 |
| Formal verification runs per workspace | 1/day | 400/day | 800/day |
| Concurrent formal verification runs | 1 | 2 | 2 |

**Uncapped** applies to graph size on Pro and Teams. Build, review, verification, and operational limits still apply.

Pro also includes deep reading of your documents. Teams adds shared graphs, centralized billing, and a **dedicated Slack support channel**. See the [Cloud overview](/platform/overview) for graphs, Wiki, reviews, formal verification, Learning, and agent memory.

### Build concurrency

All hosted plans allow **1 index build at a time per repository** and **10 across a workspace**. PR reviews and formal verification runs have the plan-specific concurrency limits shown above.

### Shared operational limits

The published hosted plans also have the following limits:

| Operation | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Memory ingestion | 2,000 ingests/day |
| Stored memory | 10,000 turns/repository |
| Memory consolidation | 1 concurrent job/workspace |
| Ask requests | 30/minute |
| Query requests | 120/minute |
| MCP requests | 240/minute |

If an operation reaches a limit, follow the message in the console. For higher capacity or a different deployment model, [discuss Enterprise](https://graphify.com/enterprise) with the team.

## Enterprise

Enterprise includes unlimited repositories and combines the graph workflow with **self-hosting, SSO/SAML, GitHub Enterprise support, formal verification, graph-aware PR review, and a dedicated Slack support channel**. It also supports custom invoicing, payment terms, a DPA, and terms of service.

Licensing is **per developer seat**. Deployment, capacity, and pricing are scoped with your account team. Enterprise is currently in **early access**.

## Billing and subscription management

Open **Billing** in your [Graphify Cloud workspace](https://app.graphify.com) to review the current plan, seats, billing interval, and renewal information. Use its billing portal for payment details, invoices, and cancellation. Billing actions depend on your workspace permissions.

Paid subscriptions renew until cancelled. Under the standard [Terms of Service](https://graphify.com/terms), cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and paid fees are non-refundable except where required by law. Review the confirmation shown when changing a plan or seat count. Enterprise invoicing and changes follow the agreement with your account team.

## Common questions

### Do I need Enterprise for formal verification?

No. Formal verification is also part of Graphify Cloud, with the hosted allowances listed above. Enterprise adds the deployment, identity, support, and commercial arrangements described in the [enterprise guide](/pricing/enterprise).

### Should we choose Teams or Enterprise?

Choose Teams for a hosted workspace with shared graphs, central billing, and a dedicated Slack support channel. Discuss Enterprise if you need your own infrastructure, SSO/SAML, GitHub Enterprise support, or negotiated procurement terms.

### Does buying more seats increase every usage limit?

The listed allowances have repository, workspace, and request scopes. Do not multiply workspace limits by seat count. Confirm any additional capacity with Graphify before planning a larger rollout.

### Where do we start?

Create a [Cloud account](https://app.graphify.com) to start Free or a paid trial. For self-hosting and enterprise requirements, [contact the enterprise team](https://graphify.com/enterprise).
